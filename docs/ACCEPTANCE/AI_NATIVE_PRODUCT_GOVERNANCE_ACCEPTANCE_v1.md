# AI-Native Product Governance — Acceptance v1

Status: REVIEW / ACCEPTANCE PLAN  
Branch: `work/ai-native-product-agency-governance-v1`

## Goal

Admit a coherent cross-product governance model for CEW/eTwin without modifying N12 engineering facts, rewriting historical acceptance evidence or advancing ETW-A0/ETW-A1.

## Required checks

### Documentation authority

- one machine-readable governance manifest identifies current authoritative models;
- v1 historical contracts remain available;
- supersession is explicit rather than silent;
- current-state files point to current governance where appropriate.

### Human System

- participant, observation, reviewer and receipt layers are separate;
- participant does not self-approve release;
- live telemetry is hidden by default;
- professional language is primary;
- critical authority/provenance errors are non-compensable;
- B1.7 v1 evidence is preserved as historical instrument evidence;
- B1 promotion remains blocked until Human Acceptance v2 is implemented and executed.

### Agentic delivery

- one promotion owner per slice;
- support agents cannot promote;
- least-authority principles are explicit;
- professional human gates cannot be bypassed;
- generated/implemented does not imply accepted/promoted.

### Product family boundary

- eTwin owns project/discipline/scope platform capabilities;
- CEW owns specialist Structures/Existing Structures workflow;
- Architecture remains autonomous and unreleased until its contract;
- N12 remains reference-project engineering authority, not product architecture.

### Historical compatibility

- ETW-A0 prepared head `36b101ed32cb61263609c84f17b740c2446be9c1` remains recorded as preparation evidence;
- eTwin v1 preparation plan/orchestration keys remain available for compatibility;
- future eTwin promotion points to v2 programme/orchestration;
- CEW promoted baseline remains unset;
- ETW-A1 remains unreleased.

### Machine validation

`python scripts/validate_product_governance.py`

Expected:

`PRODUCT_GOVERNANCE_CONSISTENCY_PASS`

Existing CEW/N12 regression gates triggered by changed current-state/program documents must also remain green. Tests must not be weakened solely to accept the governance change.

## Non-goals

- implement B1.8 UI/runtime;
- issue human HVA evidence;
- perform Production smoke;
- promote CEW B1;
- freeze `CEW_PROMOTED_BASELINE`;
- modify ETW-A0 functional code;
- promote ETW-A0;
- release ETW-A1;
- alter N12 engineering facts or professional decisions.

## Promotion after this governance tranche

The first admissible product implementation is B1.8 Human-Centred Acceptance v2. Only after B1 promotion may ETW-A0 enter v2 revalidation and platform-boundary approval.
