## Scope

Direct visual transcription of the column-reinforcement families shown for I–V ordine. The transcription preserves the distinction between family evidence and member/support binding. No reinforcement is copied from one storey/order to another.

## Canonical binding rule — carpenteria first

For member-level column reinforcement assignment, the mandatory chain is:

`CARPENTERIA DEL PIANO -> SUPPORT_ID + SECTION + ORIENTATION -> CORRESPONDING ORDER IN TAV-07A -> MATCHING REINFORCEMENT FAMILY`

Rules:

1. The storey carpenteria is the authority for the existence, identity, orientation and cross-section of a column at that level.
2. TAV-07A is the authority for reinforcement details of the family corresponding to that section and order.
3. Pillar numbers written in TAV-07A are a secondary documentary cross-check. They cannot override a conflicting section documented by the storey carpenteria.
4. A reinforcement family may be bound to a support only when the carpenteria section/orientation and the TAV-07A family are compatible. If they are not, the binding remains residual/conflict; geometry is not modified to make the reinforcement schedule fit.
5. A possible drafting typo is an interpretation, not a documentary fact. It remains `RIF_USER_QUALIFIED/INC` until independently corroborated.
6. Orientation-equivalent rectangular sections (for example 30x45 / 45x30) require an explicit orientation crosswalk from the carpenteria; they are not silently normalized.

This rule implements the cross-domain sequence already required by the canonical protocol: carpenteria defines documented structural configuration, while reinforcement sheets define details only where directly readable and correctly bound.

Binding to G1 must therefore be performed from the independently documented PT carpenteria section/orientation first, then matched to the I-order family. The family itself is DOC; member binding is not automatically direct-DOC.

Historical-model and as-built reinforcement semantics remain separate; section compatibility alone does not prove their reinforcement.
4. III-order numbered groups do not explicitly account for supports 9 and 16. Their reinforcement must be resolved from the G3 carpenteria section/orientation followed by section-family matching, not by importing the IV-order `9-16` labels.
5. IV-order supports 9 and 16 are to be bound from the IV-impalcato carpenteria section. Where the carpenteria gives 40x40, the compatible IV-order 40x40 family is the candidate; the separate 30x45 `9-16` callout remains a documentary conflict/watch and does not create a second column section.
6. IV-order supports 11 and 14 remain to be resolved by the same carpenteria-first rule. Their absence from a direct numbered IV-order group is not itself evidence that they are absent or that another numbered group should be copied to them.
7. V-order 40x40 group explicitly lists support 24 on TAV-07A, while frozen G5 carpenteria geometry excludes support 24. This remains a reinforcement-document/geometry conflict watch only; it does not trigger M0G-REOPEN without new direct carpenteria evidence.
