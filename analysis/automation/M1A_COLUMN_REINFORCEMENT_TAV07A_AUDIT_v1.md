## Canonical binding rule — carpenteria first

For member-level column reinforcement assignment, the mandatory chain is:

`CARPENTERIA DEL PIANO -> SUPPORT_ID + SECTION + ORIENTATION -> CORRESPONDING ORDER IN TAV-07A -> MATCHING REINFORCEMENT FAMILY`

Rules:

1. The storey carpenteria is the authority for the existence, identity, orientation and cross-section of a column at that level.
2. TAV-07A is the authority for reinforcement details of the family corresponding to that section and order.
3. Pillar numbers written in TAV-07A are a secondary documentary cross-check/discriminator. They cannot override a conflicting section documented by the storey carpenteria.
4. A reinforcement family may be bound to a support only when the carpenteria section/orientation and the TAV-07A family are compatible. If they are not, the binding remains residual/conflict; geometry is not modified to make the reinforcement schedule fit.
5. When only one family in the correct order is section/orientation-compatible, that unique family may be bound even if the support number is not repeated in the TAV-07A callout. When two or more reinforcement variants share the same compatible section, an explicit compatible support ID or independent evidence is required to select the variant.
6. A possible drafting typo is an interpretation, not a documentary fact. It remains `RIF_USER_QUALIFIED/INC` until independently corroborated.
7. Orientation-equivalent rectangular sections may be rotated only when the correct order contains no distinct exact-orientation competing family or when the explicit support ID disambiguates same-section variants. Orientation is always retained in the binding matrix.

This implements the canonical cross-domain sequence: carpenteria defines documented structural configuration; reinforcement sheets define reinforcement details only where correctly bound.
