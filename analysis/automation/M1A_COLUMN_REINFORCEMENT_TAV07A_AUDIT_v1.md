## Important watches

1. I ordine families are not accompanied by an explicit complete pillar-number list. Binding to G1 must therefore be by cross-validation with the independently DOC PT section/orientation catalog; the family itself is DOC, the member binding is not automatically direct-DOC.
2. G1 support 3 is documented in the current PT catalog as 60x40, while no 60x40 family is present in the current I-order transcription. Keep as an assignment residual rather than forcing a 50x40 family.
3. The 30x30 I-order family is compatible with the later terrace supports a-b-c-d by section, but those supports are known to be later additions absent from the original calculation model. Historical-model and as-built reinforcement semantics must remain separate.
4. III-order numbered groups transcribed in this pass do not explicitly account for supports 9 and 16; they remain assignment residuals until the transition detail/order semantics are resolved.
5. IV-order direct groups do not explicitly account for 11 and 14 in the main family list; keep them open pending the transition/order interpretation.
6. V-order 40x40 group explicitly lists support 24 on TAV-07A, while frozen G5 carpenteria geometry excludes support 24. This is a reinforcement-document/geometry watch only; it does not trigger M0G-REOPEN without new direct carpenteria evidence.
